### Design Inspiration - 5/5

All four top-range elements have evidence. Page 1 explicitly nominates costume and explains audience
recognition, stage distance, lighting, movement, and the two-scene change. The separate overskirt and detachable
collar are justified adaptations. They connect to the tested 48-second median change rather than remaining
an untested promise.

Page 2 compares the roles of black in mourning, evening dress, and a fitted-waist party silhouette. It explains
what is retained and rejected: restrained texture without rigid movement, repeated tiers without a dropped
waist, and volume below a defined waist. Beading and braid lead to cuff decoration and hem construction.
The screen and composite studies support those arguments and are identified as studies rather than museum
object records. The section connects context to manufacture, rather than only naming historical periods.

### Visual Design Development - 5/5

Pages 3-5 contain labelled front and back views, fabric rendering, and enlarged details. The first design
establishes recognition but is criticised for copying and the buckle's emphasis. The second tests a closed
line and dense decoration but identifies restricted movement and manufacturing time. The third combines
those ideas while exposing the fixed collar, fixed tiers, raw wrist, and lapped zip as problems.

The final design responds with a separate carrier and band, graded tiers, a detachable collar, a cuff, and an
invisible zip. Its justification uses line, rhythm, contrast, texture, proportion, and unity alongside change
time, heat, snagging, and fastening reliability. The pictures on page 5 are correctly described as views on
a form; they are not mislabelled as freehand sketches. The smaller closure details are explicitly referred
to page 6. Digital and monochrome communication is effective here.

### Manufacturing Specification - 4/5

The description, pattern modifications, production drawings, technical production plans, and product labels
are present. Pages 6-8 identify materials, measurements, cut quantities, allowances, notches, grain, construction
order, notions, prices, and completion stages. The neckline and collar arc agree, and the overskirt's geometry
and gathering schedule reconcile well. The labels distinguish the mixed-fibre dress, nylon overskirt on its
polyester band, and cotton collar. The two-dot collar iron symbol agrees with the warm-iron instruction on
page 12; the dress and overskirt symbols also agree with the component care table.

F01 prevents the highest mark for reliable scale communication. I read the unrendered front and back outlines
as scale flats because page 6 calls them flats and labels both 1:10. Under that reading, the hem shown across
each view is approximately half the width implied by its stated circumference and the pattern. If the outline
is instead intended as a projected, draped elevation, that convention and the relationship to the laid-flat
measurement need to be stated. This is a specific drawing issue, not a rejection of the detailed cut schedule.
One issue under the drawing descriptor places the section at the lower mark of 4 to 5; it does not justify
calling the whole specification elementary or applying several deductions.

### Investigation, Experimentation and Evaluation - 9/10

The nine experiments cover materials, equipment, and manufacturing processes relevant to this costume.
Their methods state useful controls and dimensions. Results include stand-off, wash performance, viewing
distance, seam drift, gathering consistency, marking removal, hem support, fastening behaviour, and change
time. Each experiment leads to a material selection or construction choice that can be traced into pages 6-8.

Page 12 connects fibre, yarn, and structure to lighting, movement, comfort, cleaning, and damage in use.
It distinguishes fabric selection from isolating a single causal variable. The text appropriately limits
the skin check, 48-hour hanging test, simulated load, and three wearer changes. The distinction between a
straight tulle seam trial and curved sleeve setting is especially clear. The median of 52, 48, and 47 seconds
is correctly reported as 48 seconds.

The shortfall is visual support for the stated specimens in Experiments 2 and 4. F02 and F03 are grouped
under the same investigation-evidence descriptor. They do not erase the result tables or the other seven
experiments. All three top-range dimensions remain substantially evidenced, including evaluation against
end-purpose, so 9 is more appropriate than the 7 to 8 range. Correcting the method-to-picture relationship
would support 10 without adding more experiments or more prose.
