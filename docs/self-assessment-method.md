# Self-assessment method

How to mark the folio the way a NESA marker would, before a marker does. The criteria and page limits are in
[nesa-folio-requirements.md](nesa-folio-requirements.md). This document is the procedure.

## What the marks are

The Major Textiles Project is worth 50 marks: 25 for the textile item and 25 for the folio. The folio's 25 split
5, 5, 5 and 10 across the four sections in their mandated order. NESA does not mark the folio in levels or bands;
it marks each section against its criteria and mark-range descriptors. "Full marks" means the top descriptor in
every section: 5, 5, 5 and 9 to 10. That is the bar every self-assessment is measured against.

The 10-mark section, Investigation, Experimentation and Evaluation, is worth as much as any two others. Mark it
first and mark it hardest.

## How to mark

1. Print the deck at true size from `scripts/preview.ps1 -Item folio`. Mark the paper, not the screen.
2. Read each section against its verbatim criteria. For every claim the folio makes, find the evidence on the
   page: the image, the measurement, the mounted sample, the table row. A claim without evidence on the page
   scores as if it were not made.
3. Follow every cross-reference. "Image 3", "Experiment 7", "page 8, step 9" must land on the thing named.
4. Re-add the costing. Check that sizes, fibre content and care agree between page 6, page 7, the labels and the
   tag.
5. Open every image and read every caption. Placeholders count as missing evidence.
6. Count pages per section against the limits. Anything past a limit is invisible to the mark.
7. Fill the compliance audit below, then the section table, then list findings in order of marks at stake.

## Findings

List each finding with the marks at stake, the page and line, and the fix. Order by marks at stake. The usual
pattern is that the writing is already top band and the marks are lost to unfinished evidence: empty mounts,
placeholder drawings, a claim that does not survive checking. Fix those before polishing prose.

Priorities:

- P0: marks directly at stake (missing evidence, breached rule).
- P1: internal consistency (a number or a name that differs between pages).
- P2: insurance and polish (a caption, a credit, a nit in the type).
- P3: print and submission logistics.
