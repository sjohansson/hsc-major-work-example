Single option for review. The chapter field enters as the pallu’s S-curve, then the same line turns and becomes the hem of the drape: it sinks below the cream band’s straight baseline and carries the full-strength Rose border all the way to the right edge, laid over the engraving rules like cloth resting on the page.

Design Inspiration

One continuous line. The border enters parallel to the top edge, falls through a symmetrical S — horizontal to horizontal, never doubling back — and runs out as a dead-straight hem to the right edge, sunk just below the cream band’s baseline. Field above the line, double rule passing beneath the cloth. Shown in Chapter 1 Rose (field #C8C9D6, border #7C7F9E); the same geometry takes the Rose/Sage chapter pairings.

Kept from the earlier study: text stays Ink; “Part I” sets as a numeral in the period line to clear the transition; fields are token tints, borders full tokens — adopting this needs the one-line brand-kit amendment for a chapter field behind the dateline.
