Printing

  1. Print this sheet at 100 per cent on A4. Do not fit to page.
  2. Matt photo or 200 gsm paper - the faces are glued down, so the stock only has to hold ink cleanly.
  3. Print a second copy before cutting. Two faces, one board and one punch leave no room to recover a slip.
Wording is not free text. The back repeats page 7's component care, and the sewn-in labels repeat it again. Change one, change all three.

Making up

  1. Cut both faces roughly oversize, leaving the outline intact.
  2. Glue the front to the board with an even, thin spread; glue the back to the reverse, squaring the two cut lines to each other.
  3. Press flat under weight until dry, then cut through all three layers on the outline - just inside the line, so no hairline survives on the edge.
  4. Punch the 5 mm hole on its circle. Punch through the board, not into it, and back the tag with scrap so the reverse does not tear out.
  5. Thread a 20 cm length of the 7 mm black grosgrain: fold it in half, pass the loop through the hole and draw the ends back through the loop. Seal both ends with a lighter.
Why board. A tag cut from paper curls and reads as a printout. Two printed faces on a 1-1.5 mm core give a card with a visible edge and enough weight to hang straight off the garment - the difference between a label and a finished object.
