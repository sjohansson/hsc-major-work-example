Sketch 1A, front view. Design One takes the 2022 school dance dress at face value. Draw a front view of a mid-calf black
dress on the croquis: a sheer organza yoke across the upper chest and shoulders with a gathered ruffle running around
the neckline, a black shirt collar with a short button placket showing through the sheer yoke, small ruffled cap
sleeves, an opaque fitted bodice below the yoke, a wide black belt with a plain rectangular metal buckle at the natural
waist, and a skirt of four uneven tiers of sheer black tulle over an opaque underdress, the tiers slightly asymmetric
and deeper towards the hem, the underdress hem just visible above the lowest tier. Feet together, arms relaxed. Style: a
Year 12 student's own fashion design development sketch. Fine black ink line over light pencil on warm off-white
cartridge paper, a faceless stylised croquis figure with simplified hands and hair pinned up, garment drawn with clear
construction lines, seams and gathers indicated with short ticks, light grey pencil-wash shading only, black garment
rendered as hatched and washed greys so seams stay legible, white pieces left as paper. Monochrome, no colour.
Hand-drawn character: slight line weight variation, one or two faint construction guidelines left visible. Leave clean
margins so numbered callouts can be added over the image in the layout. No text, numbers, arrows, labels, borders,
watermark or photographic rendering. Format: tall portrait, approximately 9:16. The printed slot is 34.7 x 66.4 mm and
shows the whole image with object-fit contain, so keep the full figure inside the frame with a small margin all round;
the figure fills about 90% of the height and is centred horizontally.
